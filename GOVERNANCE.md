# Governance

* **Maintainers** review and merge changes, cut releases and steward the
  roadmap. The initial maintainers are the members of the vizentro-labs
  organization; contributors with sustained, high-quality contributions are
  invited to become maintainers by consensus of the existing maintainers.
* **Decisions** are made by lazy consensus on issues and pull requests. A
  maintainer may call a vote if consensus fails; a simple majority of
  maintainers decides.
* **Releases** follow semantic versioning. Until 1.0 the C ABI and Rust API
  may change in minor releases; changes are listed in the release notes.
* **Ownership**: the project is owned by Vizentro Labs. Outside
  contributions are accepted under the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).
* **Code of conduct**: participants follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
  (Contributor Covenant 2.1).
